Legal Framework & Regulatory Notices

Legal, Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective Date: September 2026. Zoltraak is dedicated to user privacy, software sovereignty, and transparent computing. Please read these terms, policies, and notices governing your use of the Zoltraak desktop application, CLI, engine daemon, and browser companion extension.

01

Privacy Policy

Zoltraak is built upon a local-first architecture with no accounts and no analytics. We believe that what you download, when you download it, and which websites you visit belong exclusively to you.

No Data Collection Commitment

Zoltraak does not collect, record, transmit, sell, or monetize any personal information, device identifiers, analytics, IP addresses, browsing histories, or download manifests. The software has no remote analytics SDKs or tracking pixels.

Information Handled Strictly on Your Device

The only information processed by Zoltraak is the data necessary to execute the downloads you request:

  • Download URLs & Metadata: URLs, file names, file sizes, and destination paths you provide are stored exclusively in your local SQLite database.
  • Local IPC Traffic: All communication between the desktop interface, the CLI, and the background engine daemon is routed over your machine's local loopback network interface (127.0.0.1) and is never accessible across a local area network (LAN) or the internet.
  • Hardware Resource Profiling: System memory and CPU queries performed by the engine are used solely to schedule thread pools and parallel connection chunks locally.

No Usage Heartbeat

The desktop app does not send usage heartbeats, running-instance counts, or launch identifiers to a Zoltraak webserver. Network requests needed for features you use, such as downloads, Discord delivery, and signed update checks, remain available.

02

Local-First Data Guarantees

Zoltraak is supported on Windows. All persistent state resides solely on your computer's storage media:

  • Windows: %AppData%\Zoltraak\ (e.g., C:\Users\<Username>\AppData\Roaming\Zoltraak\)

Deleting this directory completely clears all queue registers, download histories, scheduling configurations, and custom routing rules. Uninstalling or removing Zoltraak leaves no remote remnants.

03

Cookie Vault & Session Privacy

Zoltraak includes a browser companion extension designed to synchronize authentication credentials for media sites requiring login (such as YouTube, Reddit, X, Instagram, and Facebook).

  • One Active Source: When multiple browser profiles are connected, only the profile selected under Settings → Extension → Cookie source may read or replace shared browser sessions.
  • Encryption at Rest: Session cookies received from the active browser extension are immediately encrypted using machine-bound hardware encryption before being written to the engine's internal session vault.
  • Zero Remote Transmission: Extracted session cookies are sent only to the specific third-party domain they belong to (e.g. YouTube cookies are sent only to YouTube CDN endpoints) during chunk requests. They are never transmitted to any third-party logging or aggregation servers.
  • Sanitized Logging: Cookie values, authentication tokens, and authorization headers are scrubbed from log files, terminal readouts, and UI snapshots. Only cookie names and expiration timestamps are displayed in settings.
  • Instant Revocation: You may forget and erase all stored cookies at any time under Settings → Extension → Browser sessions or by invoking zoltraak sessions forget via the CLI.
